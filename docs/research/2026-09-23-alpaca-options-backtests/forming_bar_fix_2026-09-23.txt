   The strategy keeps its own completed-bar filter and counts bars the source returned

   Before (--legacy-forming-bar, remove_incomplete_current_bar=False):
     HISTORY CHECK: 33 five-minute history calls, 33 bars returned while still forming
   After (default):
     HISTORY CHECK: 33 five-minute history calls, 0 bars returned while still forming

   Trades: both runs match alpaca_env_orb_2026_trades.csv row for row (16 rows; time, side,
   status, quantity, symbol, price). No trade changed because this strategy already dropped
   the forming bar itself and asked for one extra bar to make up for it. A strategy without
   that guard would have made its breakout decision on a close up to 5 minutes in the future.

3) weekly_call proof (options), 2026-07-27 to 2026-08-14.
