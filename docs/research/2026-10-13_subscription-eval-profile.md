# Subscription eval profile

The current candidate actor is `chatgpt/gpt-6-luna` with Medium reasoning. The
independent judge candidate is the same Luna model with XHigh reasoning. Both use the
developer's ChatGPT login and the standard/default service tier. API-key funding,
premium service tiers and automatic escalation to another model are prohibited.
This supersedes the earlier High-actor/Sol-judge proposal; it does not relabel
historical runs or qualify the candidate for release.

The earlier Medium actor result remains a failed baseline: one of five
`options_expiration_with_data` repeats confused the short contracts with their
protective wings even after reading the correct deltas. Subsequent general
contract-selection guidance changed that baseline's source. Low reasoning was
then tested first and failed a valid iron-condor selection case. Medium passed
all five targeted repetitions of that case; an isolated provider timeout was
retried while the four successful repetitions were retained. This is targeted
profile evidence, not complete publication proof. The historical expiration
failure must also pass on the final source.

The judge was tested at Low and Medium reasoning before High. Both cheaper
profiles missed a labeled calibration control. High passed an earlier calibration
catalog, but final-source calibration accepted unsafe controls involving a changed
option leg without matching evidence and a false explanation of a failed request.
Those results disqualify that profile. XHigh and stricter general judging guidance
must earn fresh calibration; they are not yet qualified. Any subsequent runtime or
qualification-helper change requires fresh source-bound actor and calibration evidence.
Changing a profile never changes an earlier failure into a pass.

The fixture obtains its actor effort from the runner's canonical constant. Each
candidate keeps one append-only call ledger covering actors, retries, judges and
calibration. Resume only results matching the current source and case fingerprint.
Historical versions, publication ceilings, permissions, risk constraints and
judge rubrics remain required. Strategy configurations using another reasoning
profile are not qualified by this profile's receipt.

The runner's cumulative ceiling is currently 2,500 calls. Every actor, judge,
nested call, failure and interrupted reservation counts in the same ledger.
An allowance amendment must be explicit and append-only; constructors cannot
silently increase it. A proposed larger allowance is not an active allowance.
Publication remains blocked until the verifier accepts the exact current source,
SDK versions, all required case repetitions and complete judge calibration.
