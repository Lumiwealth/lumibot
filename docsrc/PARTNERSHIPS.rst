Partner with LumiBot
====================

Build trading products with the team behind LumiBot.

:Last updated: September 8, 2026
:Status: Open for partnership inquiries
:Audience: Broker, market-data, wallet, and developer-platform teams

Overview
--------

LumiBot gives Python developers a shared framework for strategy logic, AI agents,
historical backtests, and broker execution. We work with teams that want their
infrastructure to become useful inside real trading workflows.

You can fund a specific integration, support ongoing open-source development,
or work with us on a broader product collaboration. Start with a concrete
developer problem and an outcome we can demonstrate together.

**Discuss a partnership:** `Email the LumiBot team <mailto:rob@botspot.trade?subject=LumiBot%20partnership>`_.

Ways to work together
---------------------

**Fund an integration or improve an existing one.**
Help developers use your brokerage, market data, wallet, or research API through
LumiBot. A scoped project can include an adapter, tests, a runnable example,
documentation, and agreed maintenance. New wallet and venue integrations require
technical scoping before we commit to supported behavior.

**Support the open-source project.**
Sponsor maintenance, broker compatibility, backtesting reliability, agent
tooling, or documentation. We agree on the work, progress reporting, and public
recognition before the engagement begins. Contributions benefit the wider
developer community under the project's license.

**Create a joint developer tutorial.**
Give your users a complete path from your API to a working strategy. We can
collaborate on a code example, a technical walkthrough, and a workshop or article
for your developer channels. Distribution and acceptance criteria belong in the
project scope.

**Explore a strategic collaboration.**
For teams considering a deeper technology or business relationship, we welcome
a confidential conversation about product integration, distribution, investment,
and other strategic opportunities.

What a first project could deliver
----------------------------------

* A stock-trading example that connects your data to agent research and broker execution.
* An options workflow with explicit contract selection, multi-leg order handling, and inspectable results.
* A macro research team that records the evidence behind portfolio decisions.
* A scoped wallet integration that connects agent decisions to transaction policies.

Each project starts with an agreed scope, prerequisites, ownership, milestones,
acceptance tests, and commercial terms. Sponsorship does not by itself grant
exclusivity, change the open-source license, or establish a support SLA.
